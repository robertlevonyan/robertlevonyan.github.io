Privacy Policy for Scanio

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Scanio ("the app") is an offline data-collection terminal for retail staff: scanning products, counting stock, checking shelf prices, and managing the documents that come out of that work. It is built and maintained by inn and itbox ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what it does not do.

In short: Scanio runs on the device it's installed on. Sign-in accounts and everything you scan or count stay on that device unless the business running the terminal deliberately configures an export destination in Settings — and even then, data goes to a server or folder that business chooses and controls, not to us. We don't run analytics, advertising, or crash-tracking in this app, and we don't sell or share your data.

Who this policy is for

Scanio is a business tool: it's installed on a shared device by a retail business for its staff to use during a shift, not distributed as a consumer app. If you're an employee using a terminal that runs Scanio, the business that gave you access to it is the one that manages your account and decides how long your data is kept — we describe below what the app itself does with information, and who to contact for what.

Information the app handles

Account information

To sign in, the app stores a username, display name, role (admin, manager, or user), and password on the device's local database. This lets staff sign in without a network connection. This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party — it stays on the device and is used only to control sign-in and what each role is allowed to do inside the app.

Scanning and inventory data

Product codes, barcodes, quantities, prices, and the documents built from them exist on the device for the length of a collection session. This data is not sent anywhere by the app on its own.

Activity log

The app keeps a short-lived log of actions taken inside it — who signed in, who changed a role, who completed a document — so a business can audit what happened on a shared terminal. This log lives on the device.

Data your organization chooses to export

Settings lets an administrator configure where completed documents get exported: a local folder on the device, or a wireless connection (FTP, SMB, or a back-office API) with its own address and credentials. If configured, exported files — the same product and quantity data described above — are sent to that destination. That destination is chosen and operated by the business running the terminal, not by us; we do not receive a copy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use the device camera or take photos — scanning is handled by a connected hardware barcode scanner, not image capture.
  • It does not access device location.
  • It does not access contacts, messages, or other apps' data.
  • It does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software.
  • It does not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data storage and retention

Accounts, scan data, and the activity log are stored locally on the device for as long as the app is installed, until an administrator removes or disables an account, or until the app's storage is cleared. Data sent to an export destination is retained according to that destination's own systems and policies, which the business running the terminal controls.

Children's privacy

Scanio is a workplace tool for retail staff and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Because accounts on a Scanio terminal are created and managed by the business that operates it, requests to view, correct, or delete an account's data should go to that business's administrator first — they can edit or disable the account directly in the app's Users screen. For questions about how the app itself works, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
