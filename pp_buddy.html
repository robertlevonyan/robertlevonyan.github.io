Kitchen Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary

Kitchen Buddy does not have user accounts, does not run its own servers, and does not collect analytics, crash reports or advertising data. What you set up stays on your device, and the app only talks to the outside services you choose to use.

This policy covers the Kitchen Buddy app for Android (package com.robertlevonyan.kitchenbuddy), published by Robert Levonyan ("I", "me"). It applies to the app as distributed on Google Play and from my website.

In short:

  • No account, no sign-up. You can use the app without giving me any personal information.

  • No tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

  • Stored on your device. Settings, favourites, timers, locations and sign-ins are kept only on the device running the app.

  • You choose the services. Optional features (Google Photos, Google Home, Home Assistant, Spotify, Apple Music) connect only after you sign in or connect them yourself, and you can disconnect them at any time.

Voice input and speech

When you tap the microphone, the app uses Android's built-in speech recognition service on your device to turn speech into text. Depending on your device, that service (often Google's) may process the audio online under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not record, store or send audio. Spoken answers use Android's built-in text-to-speech.

Sharing, selling and advertising

I do not sell, rent or share your personal information, and I have none to share: the app sends nothing to me.

  • No advertising. The app shows no ads and uses no advertising ID.

  • No analytics or crash reporting. The app contains no third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

  • No profiling. I do not combine data across services or build profiles of you.

Information only leaves your device when the app contacts the services listed above, to do what you asked.

Security, retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it, and you can remove all of it yourself at any time.

  • Security. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Online services are reached over encrypted HTTPS connections, except some free-to-air TV streams and your own Home Assistant server, which may use plain HTTP if that is how they are set up. For a Home Assistant server with its own certificate, the app trusts only the exact certificate you confirm.

  • Retention. Data is kept for as long as the app is installed, or until you remove it.

  • Disconnect a service. Use Disconnect or Unlink for that service in the app. This deletes its stored sign-in and, for Google Photos, the copied photos, and asks Google to revoke the app's access.

  • Delete everything. Clear the app's storage in Android settings (Apps → Kitchen Buddy → Storage → Clear storage), or uninstall the app.

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to delete on my side. Data held by the services you connected is managed in your account with them.

Children, your rights, changes and contact

Children

Kitchen Buddy is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). I do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU or UK under the GDPR, or in California under the CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Since I do not collect or hold personal data about you, these rights are best exercised by managing the data on your device as described above, and with the services you connected. You can also contact me with any question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the app handles information, I will update this policy and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
